Original note: Create free keys at app.alpaca.markets (paper account is fine; the data API is the same). Use the delayed feed for the premarket screen so relative volume is meaningful, then switch to IEX for live breakout tracking if you don't have the paid feed.

Screening criteria

Relative volume is today's volume so far divided by the 20-day average full-day volume. Before the open, 0.3–0.5× is already unusual; after the open the original 3× threshold applies. Market cap isn't available from Alpaca, so check it separately for anything under about $10/share.
